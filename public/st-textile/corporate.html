About ST Global Textile

About Us

25+ years of textile expertise

Founded with a vision to bring world-class apparel manufacturing & sourcing to international markets, ST Global Textile has grown into an integrated production & sourcing house serving different segment brands all over the world.

Our strength lies in more than twenty-five years of global sourcing experience across several brands — first in management roles and later as a supplier — combining strong production with skilled marketing and merchandising.

Every order is treated as a partnership — supported by transparent communication, rigorous quality assurance and commitment.
